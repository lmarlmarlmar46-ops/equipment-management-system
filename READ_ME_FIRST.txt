Hi! Welcome to EquipTrack - Equipment Management System

================================================================================
                         WHAT IS THIS?
================================================================================

EquipTrack is a complete web application for managing:
- Equipment allocation to employees
- Equipment requests and approvals
- Maintenance tracking
- Inventory management
- Asset lifecycle

Everything is ready to deploy to Railway (free hosting)!
